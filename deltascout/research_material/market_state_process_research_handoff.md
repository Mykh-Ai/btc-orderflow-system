## 1. Purpose

- Fact: this memo preserves the current shift in DeltaScout research framing.
- Fact: the shift is away from isolated rejected-`PEAK` reading and toward market-state / process-phase research.
- Fact: the memo is a compact handoff, not a batch summary, not a manifesto rewrite, and not a live-logic document.

## 2. What has been learned so far

- Fact: the Family A / Family B split remains useful.
- Fact: Family A remains the stronger transition-timing lead.
- Fact: Family B remains the broader setup-discovery lane.
- Fact: within Family B, B1 is the confirmed operational subtype.
- Fact: within Family B, B3 remains the strongest anomaly lane.
- Fact: B2 is not currently supported as a distinct subtype and is better treated as a thinner edge case inside the broader B1 lane unless stronger evidence appears.
- Fact: accepted `PEAK` short flow currently looks more like a narrow survival path than a robust stronger-structure filter.
- Fact: accepted-flow postmortem work did not support one single common immediate `SL` failure path across the accepted short `PEAK` cases.
- Interpretation: accepted flow remains useful as a diagnostics surface, but it does not currently define the strongest market structures.
- Fact: `3of3_fail` clarification reduced opacity for B1-like cases by showing that many already aligned short rejects are not weak in visible structure; they fail deeper after alignment rather than before it.
- Fact: `cum_delta_24h` must now be treated as regime context, not as a hidden background field.

## 3. Key reframing

- Fact: current research should not be framed as “find value in rejected `PEAK`s”.
- Interpretation: current research should be framed as:
  - identify market state
  - identify transition
  - identify process phase
  - identify where current `PEAK` logic sits relative to that process
- Fact: `PEAK` diagnostics still matter.
- Fact: `PEAK` remains one important direction, not the dominant center of the whole research program.
- Interpretation: rejected near-`PEAK` material is valuable mainly because it exposes recurring market behavior and hidden process structure, not because every reject is a hidden good trade.

## 4. Current working process model

- Hypothesis: a short-side market process may unfold through phases such as:
  - ground / precursor
  - manipulation / fake long picture / sweep
  - truth seed before full regime confirmation
  - post-regime-flip continuation
  - terminal continuation / late short attempt
- Interpretation: Family A often sits earlier in the process, when short pressure is forming but placement conflict is still visible.
- Interpretation: earlier Family B / B1 material often sits in post-alignment failure, where local short structure is already visible but broader regime or deeper timing still interferes.
- Interpretation: later post-regime-flip material can look structurally different again, because the broader 24h regime is no longer fighting the short and continuation candidates can appear cleaner or more terminal.
- Unknown: this process model is not yet proven as a final taxonomy; it is a current working hypothesis supported by the existing case work.

## 5. Why rejected material still matters

- Fact: rejected near-`PEAK` material can reveal:
  - pre-release structure
  - post-alignment failure
  - hidden continuation pressure
  - market-state transition before the current funnel fully accepts the move
- Interpretation: rejected rows remain valuable because they expose the market process around the move, including phases that current `PEAK` logic does not cleanly accept.
- Fact: the objective is not to rescue rejects as trades.
- Fact: the objective is to understand the market process they expose.

## 6. Role of regime context

- Fact: future research must read cases in this order:
  - regime
  - transition
  - setup pressure
  - entry timing
- Fact: this maps to:
  - `cum_delta_24h`
  - `cum_delta_180m`
  - `cum_delta_60m`
  - `ret_15m` / `ret_60m`
- Interpretation: positive 24h delta with local bearish continuation can indicate:
  - absorption
  - churn / unwind
  - fake long continuation
  - or a still-positive higher-flow backdrop that is already losing control
- Fact: later post-regime-flip work already showed that short candidates after the 24h regime turns negative can look meaningfully different from earlier B1-like cases.

## 7. Practical research direction

- Interpretation: the next research direction should be market-state-first reading rather than row-level reject reading.
- Interpretation: the most useful next lanes are:
  - market-state-first reading
  - regime-aware continuation ladders
  - pre-flip vs post-flip short processes
  - B1 / B3 contrasts as part of market-process understanding
  - `PEAK`-side diagnostics as supporting evidence, not final truth
- Interpretation: the current question is increasingly “what phase of the market process is this row inside?” rather than “is this rejected row secretly a better `PEAK`?”

## 8. Final handoff verdict

- Fact: current DeltaScout research is moving from row-level reject interpretation toward market-state / process interpretation.
- Fact: `PEAK` remains useful as a diagnostics surface.
- Interpretation: the deeper search is for recurring market behavior and state transition, not for “rejected `PEAK`s” themselves.
- Interpretation: future progress is most likely to come from identifying process phases and regime shifts, then locating where accepted and rejected `PEAK` material sits inside that broader market process.
